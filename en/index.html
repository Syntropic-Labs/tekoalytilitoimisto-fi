AI and people

AI does the repetitive work. People make the decisions.

This is how the work is divided in practice. Syntropia Oy is responsible for the quality of your bookkeeping, including the AI's work.

AI

  • reads receipts and invoices
  • books similar transactions the same way every time
  • checks that the bank statement and the books match

Accountant

  • sets the rules the AI books by
  • keeps the rules up to date and makes sure they work correctly
  • handles the cases the rules have no ready answer for

Questions

What business owners ask first.

Is your question missing? Call +358 41 327 4326 or write to hei@tekoalytilitoimisto.fi.

What is an AI accounting firm?

An accounting firm where AI does the repetitive work: it reads receipts and invoices, books them and compares the bank statement with the books. An accountant sets the rules the AI works by and keeps them up to date. Because the routine work is taken care of, your accountant has time for your questions.

Can I switch from my current accounting firm?

Yes. We collect your records from your previous accounting firm and make sure the bookkeeping continues without a break. Switching costs nothing.

Do I need accounting software?

No. You send your receipts to the portal, and we do the rest. See the portal

Can I use the service in English?

Yes. You can write to us in English. The portal is in Finnish for now, and our English guide explains every button you need. Read the guide

Who is responsible if the AI makes a mistake?

Syntropia Oy is responsible for the quality of the bookkeeping, including the work the AI does. Similar transactions are always booked by the same rules, and the software checks the reports before they are sent to the Tax Administration. The accountant keeps the rules up to date and handles the cases the rules have no ready answer for. If an error is found later, we correct it, and the correction stays visible in the books. You are responsible for the receipts and invoices you send being correct.

We are registered in the anti-money laundering supervision register of the Finnish Supervisory Agency (Lupa- ja valvontavirasto), LVV-U/128176/2026.

What is not included in the price?

Foreign trade costs €50/month + VAT. Payroll is priced in a quote. Other special reports are done only if we have agreed on them in advance.

What is a pilot customer?

During the pilot, we do your bookkeeping alongside your current bookkeeping. After that you get the same service permanently 60 % cheaper than what you pay now. Ask about a pilot place

How long is the contract?

The contract is valid until further notice, and there is no notice period. When you leave, we hand your records over to you or to your new accounting firm. Otherwise we follow the general terms of the Finnish accounting sector, TAL2023 (PDF in Finnish).

Can AI help my own business too?

Yes. Syntropia also offers AI consulting alongside bookkeeping. Read about consulting (in Finnish)
